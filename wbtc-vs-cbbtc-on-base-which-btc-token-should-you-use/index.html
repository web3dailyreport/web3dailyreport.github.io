WBTC vs cbBTC on Base: Which BTC Token Should You Use?

For WBTC vs cbBTC on Base, cbBTC is the quick default: Coinbase backs it and most Base apps support it; choose WBTC when a specific market requires it.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 27, 2026 · 23:13 UTC2 min read

cbBTC is usually the right BTC token for Base: Coinbase issues it there, and most Base apps support it. Use WBTC when a specific app or position requires that token.

How does each BTC token reach Base?

To get cbBTC on Base, Coinbase converts eligible BTC sent from a Coinbase account into cbBTC at a 1:1 ratio. Coinbase holds the backing BTC, and both cbBTC and WBTC use 8 decimals.

The Base bridge moves supported assets from Ethereum to Base, where the receiving token may be a bridged version. The OP Stack’s role in Base bridge withdrawals adds useful context on why withdrawing back to Ethereum has its own timing.

WBTC is backed by BTC held in BitGo-led custody. WBTC’s redemption process runs through approved merchants; retail holders generally exit by swapping WBTC for another asset. Coinbase lets eligible customers redeem cbBTC for BTC by depositing it to a Coinbase account, where it is converted and credited as BTC.

Which token should you use?

Choose cbBTC for a typical Base lending or trading position, unless the app specifies WBTC. For lending, the market must list the exact token contract you hold: a matching ticker alone is not enough.

Say you want to supply BTC to a lending app. If its market accepts cbBTC, you can send cbBTC there and later deposit it to Coinbase to receive BTC back. If the position requires WBTC, use that token instead and plan to swap it when you want to exit as a retail holder.

WBTC sent through the Base bridge arrives as a bridged version, so confirm that the app accepts that exact contract before sending. Coinbase’s native cbBTC route avoids adding that bridge layer when you start with BTC in a Coinbase account.

Before supplying either token, open the app’s market details and compare its contract with the token shown in Basescan. That quick match helps avoid sending an asset the market will not accept.
