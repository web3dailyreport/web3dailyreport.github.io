Coinbase’s OCC Trust Bank Charter Takes Effect

Coinbase’s national trust bank charter became effective Nov. 6, putting its custody business under federal oversight while stopping short of deposit-taking and lending.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 11, 2026 · 20:25 UTC2 min read

Coinbase’s national trust bank charter took effect Nov. 6, giving the exchange a federal trust-bank status under the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), according to the OCC’s filing record. Coinbase said the OCC had elevated its previously conditional approval to a full charter, CoinDesk reported Nov. 9.

What did the OCC approve?

The regulator’s record lists Coinbase National Trust Company as a national bank charter under a holding company, with trust powers requested. It shows the application was received Oct. 3, 2025, approved April 2, 2026, and consummated or made effective Nov. 6.

The April decision was conditional, CoinDesk reported; Coinbase said the OCC signed off on the full charter after the company built compliance systems, hired staff and underwent regulatory review. The OCC filing record confirms the effective date, while Coinbase’s announcement of full status was reported by CoinDesk.

Does the charter make Coinbase a full-service bank?

No. The charter covers trust-bank operations, and Coinbase is not authorized by it to take deposits or make loans, CoinDesk reported. The outlet said the conditional approval had allowed Coinbase to offer federally regulated custody and payments services; the full charter moves that trust-bank business to its final status.

The distinction matters for customers and institutions using Coinbase’s custody services: the trust company is under the OCC’s federal banking framework, but the charter does not turn the exchange into a conventional deposit-taking bank. Coinbase Vice Chairman Ryan VanGrack described the approval as a step toward integrating digital assets into U.S. financial infrastructure, according to CoinDesk.

What happens next?

The charter is now effective, according to the OCC’s filing record. Coinbase has not announced a further regulatory milestone in the reports reviewed; the OCC record identifies Coinbase National Trust Company as an effective national trust bank.
