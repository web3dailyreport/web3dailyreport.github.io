Binance rolls out free AI interface after 150,000-user beta

Binance has rolled out its free AI trading interface to English-language users after about 150,000 people tested it, with more language support still to come.

Blockfront Editorial

Oct 13, 2026 · 11:31 UTC2 min read

Binance rolled out its free Binance AI interface to global English-language users on Oct. 12, adding personalized market information and trading tools after a beta with about 150,000 testers. The Block reported the rollout and beta figure, which a Binance spokesperson provided to the publication.

The beta began Sept. 7, according to The Block. Binance said the interface tailors information and tools to users’ interests and experience levels; support for additional languages is still rolling out.

What does Binance AI offer users?

Binance describes it as a personal assistant for interpreting market and portfolio information. Its features include AI-generated portfolio summaries, analysis of top-moving assets, voice alerts and market briefings in text or audio, The Block reported.

Binance’s Oct. 5 product announcement also describes a Market Brief covering crypto, stocks and macro updates, along with portfolio summaries and alerts. Binance said the interface is free and designed for users at different experience levels.

Does the interface execute trades automatically?

Binance AI’s rollout is separate from AI Pro, which the company describes as a tool for building automated trading workflows. Binance said users must review and approve a workflow before activation, and it can trade only within the permissions, budgets and risk limits they set.

In its Oct. 5 announcement, Binance said AI Pro would follow in the coming weeks, with a free version using credits refreshed weekly and a paid subscription planned later. The company’s beta announcement says AI-assisted trading actions are automated and Binance does not provide trading strategies or recommendations.
