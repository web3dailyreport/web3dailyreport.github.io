What does “settled” mean in CoW Explorer: Your order executed

What does settled mean in CoW Explorer? It means your order was executed on-chain; compare requested and executed amounts to see how much traded.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 18, 2026 · 15:18 UTC2 min read

“Settled” in CoW Explorer means your order was executed in an on-chain settlement. CoW Swap orders are collected and executed through batch auctions. A partially fillable order can settle in portions, so the status alone does not tell you whether the full amount traded.

CoW Explorer combines off-chain orderbook data with on-chain transaction data. Open the order using its order ID to see your order’s requested and executed amounts, then use the settlement transaction hash to inspect the on-chain batch. For CoW Swap, these steps to find a completed swap show where to locate the order in Explorer.

What happens before an order is settled?

A signed order enters the off-chain orderbook while it awaits execution. Solvers bring orders together in a batch auction, and an executed batch is recorded on-chain in a settlement transaction.

That transaction can execute multiple users’ orders at once. Its hash identifies the batch transaction, so it may be shared by your order and other orders in the same settlement.

How do you confirm what your order traded?

Open the order details by its order ID and compare the requested amounts with the executed amounts. The order’s details show your trade within the batch; the transaction hash takes you to the on-chain settlement that included it.

Say you placed an order to sell 100 tokens for another asset. If the order details show 60 tokens executed, the settlement included a trade for those 60 tokens, even if the batch transaction also handled other users’ swaps.

For a partially fillable order, execution can happen across multiple auctions. If 60 of the 100 tokens execute in one settlement, the remaining 40 may still be available for later execution. Look at the order’s amounts and settlement details to understand what has traded so far.

Use the order ID to check your own execution, and the settlement transaction hash to inspect the batch that recorded it.
