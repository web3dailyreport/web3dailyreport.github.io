Insufficient-funds-for-gas errors: Why tokens cannot pay fees

An insufficient funds for gas error can appear while your wallet holds tokens: fees require native ETH on Ethereum or SUI on Sui, on the transaction’s network.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 25, 2026 · 05:15 UTC3 min read

An insufficient-funds-for-gas error means the wallet cannot cover the transaction’s maximum fee with the native coin on that network. Find the right network and add ETH for Ethereum or SUI for Sui, or lower the amount you are sending.

Three common causes and fixes

The error usually comes from a missing native coin, a balance on the wrong network, or too little balance for the transaction’s fee cap.

No native coin on the transaction network

A wallet holding only USDT or WETH has no ETH to pay an Ethereum gas fee. The same applies on Sui: tokens there cannot replace the SUI required for gas.

Suibridge is a cross-chain bridge that locks supported assets on Ethereum and mints them on Sui. For a USDT deposit, keep ETH on Ethereum mainnet first; these Suibridge transfer checks cover that source-network requirement.

Add the native coin to the network where you will submit the transaction. A Suibridge deposit from Ethereum mainnet needs ETH on Ethereum mainnet, even when the wallet also shows USDT.

Native coin on another network

ETH on Arbitrum or Base cannot pay gas for an Ethereum mainnet transaction. A wallet may show both balances under one account, but each network checks its own balance.

Switch to the transaction’s network and check its native-coin balance. For mainnet gas, fund the Ethereum mainnet balance; ETH held on an L2 does not count toward it.

Native balance below the fee cap

Ethereum checks that the account can cover the gas limit multiplied by the maximum fee, plus any ETH value sent. A balance can look sufficient but still fall short of that maximum.

When sending ETH, reduce the send amount so the remaining balance covers the displayed maximum fee. Leave enough ETH for the wallet’s fee cap, which can vary with the transaction and network conditions.

Contact the wallet provider or transaction service if the correct network has enough native coin for the displayed maximum fee and the error continues. Include the network and transaction details so support can check the issue.

Next questions

How much ETH should I keep for gas on Ethereum?

Keep enough ETH on the same Ethereum network to cover the maximum fee shown for the transaction. The amount varies with the gas limit and max fee, so check the wallet’s estimate before sending.

How do I get SUI for gas if I only hold bridged tokens?

Acquire native SUI on the Sui network and keep it in the wallet that will submit the transaction. Bridged tokens do not pay Sui gas fees.
