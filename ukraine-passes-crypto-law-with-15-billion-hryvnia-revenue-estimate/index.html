Ukraine passes crypto law with 15 billion hryvnia revenue estimate

Ukraine’s parliament approved a crypto market and tax framework, projecting up to 15 billion hryvnias in revenue; the law is due to take effect in July 2027.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 26, 2026 · 05:25 UTC2 min read

Ukraine’s Verkhovna Rada passed a crypto market and tax bill on Wednesday, with 271 lawmakers voting in favor and eight against, according to Interfax-Ukraine’s report on the vote. Lawmaker Yaroslav Zhelezniak estimated crypto taxes could add 15 billion hryvnias, about $330 million, to the state budget, CoinDesk reported, but did not specify a time frame.

How the bill taxes crypto income

The law taxes individuals on their annual profit from virtual assets at the standard personal income tax rate, according to Interfax-Ukraine. It exempts swaps between crypto assets, profits below one minimum wage and tokens received for free.

For assets already held, the bill provides a transitional 5% personal income tax rate through the end of 2028. CoinDesk reports that a 5% military levy also applies during that period; after it ends, the rates rise to 18% personal income tax plus the levy.

Issuing and exchanging virtual assets will be exempt from value-added tax, while service providers cannot use Ukraine’s simplified tax system, Interfax-Ukraine reported.

When the rules take effect

The law is scheduled to take effect on July 1, 2027, according to Interfax-Ukraine. Exchanges, custodians and other virtual asset service providers will need authorization, register within 60 days and report annually on transactions involving Ukrainian residents.

The bill also sets rules for safeguarding client assets and protecting their rights. CoinDesk reported that the legislation still requires the president’s signature before it becomes law.
