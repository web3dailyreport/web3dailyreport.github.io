How to unwrap WETH to ETH and get ETH in your wallet

How to unwrap WETH to ETH: use a wallet or DEX Unwrap button, or call WETH9 withdraw directly. Get the same amount of ETH back, minus gas.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 28, 2026 · 05:53 UTC3 min read

Use a wallet or DEX’s Unwrap button, or call WETH9’s withdraw function to turn WETH into ETH. Ethereum.org explains that WETH is backed 1:1 by ETH held in the contract, so the unwrapped amount returns as ETH; you pay gas for the transaction.

Have WETH in your wallet, a little native ETH for gas, and the correct chain selected. WETH uses different contract addresses across networks. On standard OP Stack chains, the WETH9 predeploy is 0x4200000000000000000000000000000000000006. A cross-chain route finder such as lifi bridge helps users compare ways to move assets between chains; its route aggregation is a useful example of how a tool can show options when the task involves more than a simple unwrap.

How do you unwrap WETH to ETH?

For the fastest route, use an explicit Unwrap action; for a direct contract call, use the WETH contract on the selected chain.

  1. Choose the route. Open a wallet or DEX such as Uniswap and select its WETH-to-ETH Unwrap action, or open the verified WETH contract on that network’s block explorer. You should see the WETH balance and the available action.
  2. Enter the amount. The wallet or DEX accepts a human-readable amount. On Etherscan’s Write Contract tab, connect your wallet and call withdraw(uint wad) on the correct WETH contract; enter the amount in wei, with 18 decimals. For 0.5 WETH, enter 500000000000000000.
  3. Review and confirm. Check the chain, contract, amount, and gas in your wallet, then confirm the transaction. A direct withdrawal needs no token approval; it does need enough native ETH to pay gas.
  4. Verify the result. After confirmation, your ETH balance should rise and your WETH balance should fall by the same amount, apart from gas spent. The conversion is 1 WETH to 1 ETH; the cost is the transaction’s gas.

For a wallet or DEX, use the explicit Unwrap action rather than a market swap: it calls the WETH withdrawal mechanism and has no price impact. On an L2, use that chain’s WETH contract and explorer. Don’t assume an Ethereum contract address works on another network.

Questions users ask next

Is unwrapping WETH a taxable event?

Tax treatment depends on your jurisdiction and circumstances. The 1:1 conversion alone does not establish how your tax authority treats it; check local rules or ask a tax professional.

Can I unwrap WETH if I have zero ETH for gas?

No, not with a standard on-chain withdrawal from that wallet: the transaction needs native ETH for gas. Get a small amount of the chain’s native token first.

Why doesn’t my WETH show in my wallet?

Check that the wallet is on the network where the WETH is held and that it displays the correct WETH token contract for that chain. Add the token using its verified contract address if needed.
