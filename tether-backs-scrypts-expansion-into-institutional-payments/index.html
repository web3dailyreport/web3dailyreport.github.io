Tether backs SCRYPT’s expansion into institutional payments

Tether announced a strategic investment in Swiss stablecoin provider SCRYPT on Nov. 19, backing its move into fiat accounts and third-party payments for institutions.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 22, 2026 · 14:11 UTC2 min read

Tether made a strategic investment in Swiss stablecoin trading and liquidity provider SCRYPT on Nov. 19, saying the backing will support the firm’s expansion into third-party payments. The Tether announcement says the plan targets banks, brokers, fintechs and payment companies seeking to use stablecoins.

SCRYPT’s platform combines payments, trading, custody and asset management across fiat currencies, stablecoins and digital assets, according to Tether. It offers spot, over-the-counter and foreign-exchange trading around the clock, alongside segregated custody in Switzerland secured with multi-party computation technology.

The firms describe a cross-border payment route in which fiat currency is converted to stablecoins, transferred across borders, then converted back to fiat. They say this could let payment firms shift liquidity between markets in minutes instead of keeping funds in pre-funded accounts, and allow businesses to settle outside banking hours. Those are the companies’ stated aims, not reported results from the new investment.

SCRYPT founder and CEO Norman Wooding said the company plans to expand into fiat accounts and payments. Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino said the investment reflects the company’s view that banks and payment firms need to use stablecoins within their existing systems. Neither company disclosed the investment amount or terms; Fintech News Switzerland’s report also noted that financial terms were not disclosed.

The announcement sets out no launch date for the expanded payment services. SCRYPT’s next stated step is to build out fiat accounts and third-party payments on top of its existing liquidity, foreign-exchange and cross-border settlement infrastructure.
