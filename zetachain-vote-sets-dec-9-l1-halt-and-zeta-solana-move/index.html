ZetaChain Vote Sets Dec. 9 L1 Halt and ZETA Solana Move

ZetaChain holders approved a 1:1 ZETA migration to Solana, with the L1 set to halt at a snapshot block expected around Dec. 9, 2026.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 9, 2026 · 11:06 UTC2 min read

ZetaChain holders approved Proposal 2 on Nov. 7, setting a snapshot and L1 halt expected around Dec. 9, 2026, as ZETA moves to Solana in a 1:1 migration, according to the ZetaChain announcement. The project said more than 99.99% voted in favor.

What does the vote set for ZetaChain’s L1?

The chain will halt at block 20,755,000, which ZetaChain expects to arrive around Dec. 9. The snapshot at that height will record liquid, staked and unbonding balances, plus unclaimed staking rewards; the chain will not restart, the proposal says.

The vote was the second governance step: Proposal 68 approved the migration direction in September, while Proposal 2 set the timetable and process. Solana Compass reported a final tally of about 313.63 million ZETA in favor and 420.5 against.

How will holders receive ZETA on Solana?

ZetaChain says it will mint a native Solana token at a 1:1 ratio from the snapshot total, then revoke the mint authority. Claims are due to open on Dec. 9 through ZetaHub’s migration portal; eligible holders will connect the wallet that held ZETA and name a Solana wallet.

The proposal includes liquid, staked and unbonding balances in the snapshot. ZetaChain says exchange balances will convert automatically, while holders with ZETA in wrapped or pooled positions should exit those positions before the snapshot because contract-held tokens cannot be claimed by a key.

What happens to supply after the migration?

About 55 million ZETA in undistributed validator emissions will be burned monthly through 2027, according to ZetaChain. The project says the Solana token will have no mint authority after the snapshot supply is minted.

The next steps are publication of the Solana mint and migration program addresses and a security review report before Dec. 9, ZetaChain said. The halt will occur when the chain reaches block 20,755,000, so the date remains an estimate.
