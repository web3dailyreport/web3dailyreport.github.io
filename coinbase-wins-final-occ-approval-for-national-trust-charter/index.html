Coinbase wins final OCC approval for national trust charter

Coinbase’s national trust charter took effect Nov. 6, placing its custody business under federal oversight and opening a path to expand institutional services.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 10, 2026 · 12:21 UTC2 min read

Coinbase received final approval for a national trust charter from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), putting its custody business under federal oversight. The company said the OCC approved the charter on Nov. 6, according to its announcement of the Coinbase National Trust Company.

When did Coinbase’s trust charter take effect?

The OCC’s application record lists Coinbase National Trust Company as approved on April 2, then consummated and effective on Nov. 6. The filing identifies it as a national bank charter under a holding company in the trust bank category.

The April action was preliminary and conditional. Coinbase said it spent the intervening months meeting the regulator’s requirements before receiving final approval.

What services does the charter cover?

The charter lets Coinbase provide digital asset custody, settlement and stablecoin reserve management under the OCC’s federal framework, Banking Dive reported. Coinbase said the approval allows it to expand its custody business under consistent federal oversight.

The trust charter does not make Coinbase a full-service bank that takes deposits and makes loans, CoinDesk reported. Coinbase National Trust Company will start with its core custody offering, with other versions of that model expected to follow, according to an interview Banking Dive conducted with Rick Schonberg, the company’s head of trading and custody.

Coinbase said the federal charter builds on its work with the New York Department of Financial Services, which granted Coinbase Custody Trust Company a New York limited-purpose trust charter in 2018. The company said it will continue working with the state regulator.

The OCC’s record shows the charter is now effective; Coinbase said it plans to expand custody services under the federal framework.
