NYSE selects Avalanche for tokenized stock settlement

NYSE picked Avalanche to record and settle trades for its planned tokenized-securities venue, designed for 24/7 operation subject to regulatory approval.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 9, 2026 · 22:07 UTC2 min read

The New York Stock Exchange selected Avalanche on Nov. 9 as the ledger for clearing and settling trades on its planned tokenized-securities platform, a step toward a 24/7 market pending regulatory approval, Ava Labs said in its announcement. The NYSE-affiliated venue is planned to trade tokenized securities, The Block reported.

What will Avalanche handle?

Avalanche will provide the core ledger technology to the platform’s clearing firm, Ava Labs said. The ledger will record and settle trades; Ava Labs chief executive Emin Gün Sirer said the infrastructure is designed to support atomic settlement around the clock.

The Block reported that NYSE plans to use its existing technology to match trades. The platform’s trading, clearing and settlement are subject to regulatory approvals, according to Ava Labs.

Will the equities be issued on Avalanche?

Not necessarily: NYSE’s Head of Digital Issuance Dennis Lee said the platform will support equities listed on a range of blockchains. Avalanche is the ledger selected to record and settle trades, according to Ava Labs.

Lee said the settlement ledger needs to be fast and reliable, keep costs predictable and let NYSE control who can participate. Ava Labs said the platform is being designed for its subscribers.

When could the venue open?

The platform is designed for 24/7 trading, clearing and settlement, but Ava Labs said it needs applicable regulatory approvals. The Block reported that the venue is targeting a fourth-quarter launch, citing The Wall Street Journal; no specific launch date was given in the announcement.

The selection followed about a year of testing, The Block reported, citing Ava Labs President Charley Cooper. NYSE has said it is evaluating different technologies for different parts of the platform.
