Polygon Publishes Heimdall v0.14.0 After Purley Activations

Polygon made Heimdall v0.14.0 public on Nov. 20 after Purley activated on mainnet and Amoy, adding authenticated validator votes and bridge checks.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 21, 2026 · 06:52 UTC2 min read

Polygon Labs released Heimdall v0.14.0 for mainnet and Amoy on Nov. 20, after the Purley hard fork activated at mainnet block 58,280,000, according to its release announcement. The public version is functionally identical to the private build used for activation, the company said.

What did Purley change in Heimdall?

At the Purley activation height, Heimdall began carrying validator vote extensions as signed and authenticated data in blocks that include them. The release also requires bridge events to be confirmed before they are acted on, according to Polygon.

Purley admits staking updates only at a validator’s next nonce and keeps an expired checkpoint buffer until an approved checkpoint replaces it. Polygon also said cancelled governance proposals without a destination set are no longer charged.

When did the changes activate on each network?

Polygon reported that mainnet activated Purley at block 58,280,000 at 01:39:38 UTC on Nov. 20. Amoy activated earlier, at block 52,108,000 at 15:41 UTC on Nov. 12. Both networks had passed their activation heights by the time v0.14.0 was released.

The release also includes changes that are not gated by the activation height: Heimdall rejects staking messages with a nil amount during basic validation, and the bridge machinery worker queue is bound under its own name. Packaging templates leave the external-address field empty, Polygon said.

What must Heimdall node operators do?

Polygon said every node must run a compatible version: v0.14.0-beta-priv or later on Amoy, and v0.14.0-priv or later on mainnet. The public v0.14.0 release meets that requirement.

The company describes the update as a plain binary upgrade, with no configuration changes, state migration or resynchronization required. It said no Bor change is required for Purley; the announcement does not give a deadline for operators to install the public release.
