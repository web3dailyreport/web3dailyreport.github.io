US wins court authority over $1.2bn in Binance theft

The Justice Department has court authority over more than $1.2 billion of crypto stolen from Binance, part of $2.7 billion frozen as recovery efforts continue.

Blockfront Editorial

Nov 16, 2026 · 07:12 UTC2 min read

The Justice Department said Nov. 12 that it had obtained court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency stolen from Binance. Its announcement says the amount is part of a larger pool frozen through U.S. government and private-sector efforts.

The distinction matters: the authorization covers funds already frozen, and does not mean the government has completed forfeiture or returned the crypto. The Justice Department said efforts to trace, freeze, seize and forfeit the assets remain underway as the attackers move funds through cryptocurrency bridges, mixers and exchanges.

  • More than $2.7 billion frozen: The government and private-sector partners had frozen this much of the stolen assets by Nov. 12, according to the Justice Department.
  • More than $1.2 billion targeted by court order: That is the portion for which the department said it has authority to seize or restrain the funds.
  • More than $6 billion stolen: The Justice Department said the theft occurred on or about Oct. 29. The Associated Press reported the FBI put the theft at about $6.3 billion.

The FBI has attributed the Binance theft to TraderTraitor, a group it describes as members of the North Korean military. The Justice Department said law enforcement agencies have worked with virtual asset service providers to interdict the stolen funds.

Forfeiture proceedings will determine who has a legitimate claim to recovered crypto, the Associated Press reported. The department said efforts to track and recover the assets are ongoing.
